📘 How-To Guide & Workout Instructions

Program Rules:

  • Warm-up: Perform 5 minutes of light cardio or dynamic stretches before lifting.
  • Progressive Overload: Increase weight slightly each week if form remains solid.
  • Rest Intervals: Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.
  • Deloading (Week 4): Lower weights by 20% to let joints and muscles fully recover.

Fatigue & Burnout Scale:

  • Energy (Pre-workout): 1 = Exhausted, 5 = Peak Energy.
  • Fatigue (Post-workout): 1 = Very Light, 5 = Maximum Exhaustion.
⚠️ Alert: Consecutive high-fatigue sessions detected! Consider taking a deload day or focusing on light recovery.

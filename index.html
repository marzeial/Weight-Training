Beginner Guide & Principles

Everything you need to feel confident, safe, and empowered while lifting.

1. Understanding RPE

RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) on a scale of 1-10 measures how hard a set feels. For this beginner program, aim for a comfortable 6—where you finish feeling like you could easily do 3-4 more reps.

2. Progressive Overload

Growth happens gradually. When 10 reps start feeling easy or smooth across all sets, consider bumping the weight up by the smallest available increment next session.

3. Rest & Recovery

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets. This gives your muscles enough time to replenish energy so your form stays crisp and safe throughout every single set.
